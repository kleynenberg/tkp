Privacy Policy (EU GDPR)

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Domain: https://tkp.kleynenberg.com (podcast productions, essays, and client-side AI exploration tools)

1. Data Controller (Art. 13(1)(a) GDPR)

Steven Ernst Kleynenberg / Kleynenberg Publishers
Location: Delft, The Netherlands
Contact: steven@kleynenberg.com
Domain: https://tkp.kleynenberg.com

This website is a static, zero-tracking publication and software portal. We do not sell personal data, deploy behavioral advertising trackers, build user profiles, or operate visitor accounts.

2. Personal Data Processed & Legal Basis (Art. 6 GDPR)

  • Static Web Hosting (GitHub Pages): Hosted on GitHub Pages (https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection). When you request a page, GitHub transiently processes technical connection metadata (IP address, user-agent, timestamp) to serve static files securely. Legal basis: legitimate interest in secure network operation (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Web Typography (Google Fonts): Pages load Atkinson Hyperlegible (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Atkinson+Hyperlegible) and Noto Sans SC (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Noto+Sans+SC) via Google Fonts (https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy) for visual accessibility. Legal basis: legitimate interest in accessible legibility (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Local-First & AI-Assisted Browser Processing (Art. 22 GDPR & EU AI Act): Interactive search, visualization, and AI-assisted client tools run locally inside your browser without transmitting queries to Kleynenberg Publishers, and perform no automated legal decision-making under Article 22 GDPR.

3. EU Regulatory Framework & Your Rights (Articles 15–21 GDPR)

Governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj), the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj), and the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj). You may request access, rectification, erasure, or restriction via steven@kleynenberg.com, or lodge a complaint with the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or any EU supervisory authority coordinated via the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) (https://www.edpb.europa.eu/).
