Privacy Policy (GDPR) & Privacybeleid (AVG)

Effective date / Ingangsdatum: 24 November 2026 · Applicable to https://tkp.kleynenberg.com

1. Data Controller

Steven Ernst Kleynenberg / Kleynenberg Publishers
Waterloop #1, 2514 XC Delft, The Netherlands
Domain: https://tkp.kleynenberg.com

This website is a static, zero-tracking publication index and open-source software portal. We do not sell personal data, operate advertising trackers, run behavioral profiling, or maintain visitor accounts.

2. Personal Data Processed & Legal Basis (Art. 6 GDPR)

  • Static Web Hosting Logs (GitHub Pages): This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection), operated by GitHub, Inc. When you request a page, GitHub transiently processes technical connection metadata (such as your IP address, user-agent string, and timestamp) to deliver static assets securely and prevent abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in secure network operation (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Web Typography (Google Fonts): To ensure visual legibility and accessibility, pages load the Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface from Google Fonts (https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy). Your browser connects directly to Google servers, transmitting your IP address for font delivery. Legal basis: legitimate interest in accessible presentation (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Local-First Client Execution: Interactive browser applications, visualizations, and search interfaces hosted under https://tkp.kleynenberg.com execute locally inside your browser without transmitting search queries to Kleynenberg Publishers.

3. Data Retention & International Transfers

Kleynenberg Publishers maintains no server-side visitor database and retains zero personal logs of its own. Infrastructure providers (GitHub, Inc. and Google LLC) process technical delivery logs under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/) and standard contractual safeguards.

4. Your Rights Under the GDPR (Articles 15–21)

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj), you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, or object to processing of your personal data, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
