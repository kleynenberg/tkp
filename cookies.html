Cookie Policy (EU ePrivacy Directive)

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Domain: https://tkp.kleynenberg.com

Zero Tracking Cookies — No Cookie Banner Required
https://tkp.kleynenberg.com sets zero tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party behavioral analytics identifiers.

1. Cookies vs. Strictly Local Browser Storage

  • HTTP Cookies: Zero non-essential or tracking cookies are placed by Kleynenberg Publishers.
  • Cache Storage, Service Workers & IndexedDB: Client-side Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) on this domain use standard HTML5 Cache Storage (window.caches), Service Workers, and local IndexedDB/localStorage strictly on your device to cache static assets and UI state. Nothing is transmitted to Kleynenberg Publishers.

2. Legal Basis (EU ePrivacy Directive & Dutch Telecommunications Act)

Under Article 5(3) of the EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj) and Article 11.7a(3) of the Dutch Telecommunications Act (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0009950), consent popups are required only for non-essential tracking storage. Because zero tracking cookies are used, no intrusive consent banner is shown.

Nederlands — Cookiebeleid (Telecommunicatiewet art. 11.7a)

Op https://tkp.kleynenberg.com worden geen trackingcookies, advertentiecookies of analytische volgcookies geplaatst (https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/themas/internet-slimme-apparaten/cookies).

  • Uitsluitend lokale functionele opslag: Webapplicaties op dit domein gebruiken alleen lokale browseropslag (Cache Storage, Service Workers, localStorage en IndexedDB) op jouw eigen apparaat voor snelle laadtijden en offline werking.
  • Geen cookiebanner nodig: Op grond van artikel 11.7a lid 3 Telecommunicatiewet (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0009950) en de EU ePrivacy-richtlijn (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj) is toestemming niet vereist bij afwezigheid van trackingcookies.

https://tkp.kleynenberg.com setzt keine Tracking-, Werbe- oder Analyse-Cookies. Lokaler Browser-Speicher (Cache Storage, Service Worker, localStorage) wird ausschließlich funktional auf Ihrem eigenen Endgerät genutzt (Art. 5 Abs. 3 Richtlinie 2002/58/EG: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj).
