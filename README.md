# The Kleynenberg Perspective (`TKP`)

Podcast and commentary on technology, philosophy, strategic logic, and sports tactics, produced by Steven Kleynenberg.

**Live site:** https://tkp.kleynenberg.com (also via `https://naaap.io/tkp` on `naaap.io` — Network for Autonomous Audiovisual & Algorithmic Publishing)

## Themes

* **Technology & Systems:** Practical engineering, open architectures, and critical examination of modern tooling.
* **Philosophy & Epistemology:** Grounded reasoning, truth, clarity, and intellectual honesty.
* **Strategic Logic:** Decision-making, trade-offs, patterns, and long-term structure.
* **Sports Tactics:** Game analysis, positioning, and strategic execution.
